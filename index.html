Infoblox IQ · 2026

Making an AI assistant safe enough to ship to security teams

A context-blind guardrail was blocking legitimate requests and missing risky ones. I redesigned the classifier and drove it from proof of concept to production.

Problem, approach, outcome
ProblemSingle-pass guardrail at 74% block accuracy. Customers in regulated environments would not enable the assistant.
What I didAnalysed production conversations in Databricks, tested 41 agent threads, filed 13 triaged defects. Specified a two-pass, context-aware LLM classifier and partnered with engineering, data and security from PoC to enforcement.
Outcome~94% block accuracy in production. Unblocked GA readiness across 30+ epics spanning agents, actions, guardrails and observability.

Infoblox IQ · 2026

Letting customers embed the assistant in their own agent stacks

Enterprise SOC teams wanted Infoblox IQ inside Slack and ServiceNow workflows, not in another console. I drove the MCP agent endpoint that made that possible.

Problem, approach, outcome
ProblemNo way for customer agents to call Infoblox IQ with per-user identity and safe writes.
What I didDefined tool contracts, execution flows, per-user OAuth and human-approved writes. Reviewed authorization behaviour against the implementation and caught a defect that allowed access when checks failed, then a regression that blocked legitimate access.
OutcomeValidated a Slack → IQ → ServiceNow SOC proof of concept on Kubernetes. Built the first customer-level AI COGS model, which showed QA traffic was 97% of measured inference cost.
